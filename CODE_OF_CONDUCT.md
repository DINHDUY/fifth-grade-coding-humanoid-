# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus feedback on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Make room for different perspectives and levels of technical experience.
- Prioritize student safety, privacy, accessibility, and inclusion.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical.
